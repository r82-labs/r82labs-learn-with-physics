float r82labs::learn_with_physics::Slingshot::get_launch_velocity ( float draw_length_meters,
float mass_kg ) const

Calculates the launch velocity of a projectile.

The velocity is calculated using the formula:

\[ v = d \sqrt{\frac{\eta k}{m}} \]

where \( d \) is the draw length, \( \eta \) is efficiency, \( k \) is stiffness, and \( m \) is mass.

Parameters
draw_length_metersDistance the band is pulled back.
mass_kgMass of the projectile being launched.
