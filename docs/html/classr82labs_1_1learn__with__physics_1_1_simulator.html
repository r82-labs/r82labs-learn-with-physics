Calculates the position of a projectile at a specific time.

Using standard projectile motion equations:

\[ x(t) = v_0 \cos(\theta) t \]

\[ y(t) = v_0 \sin(\theta) t - \frac{1}{2} g t^2 \]

Parameters

projThe Projectile being simulated.
